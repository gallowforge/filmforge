The Lantern Moth · s02 · prompt
forge-painter

Her last match dies at the cold wick

01Look
A frame from a hand-painted storybook film, in exactly the style of the attached sheets: soft gouache and watercolour on rough paper, confident brush-ink outlines, visible paper grain, deep night colours, light that comes only from lamps, embers and the sky.
02References
References: the attached sheet of the girl in the red knitted hood governs her face, hood, coat and proportions; the attached sheet of the ember moth governs its material, wing shape and size. Relight both to this scene; do not copy the sheets’ flat light.
03Shot
06Setting
A medium shot inside the cold lamp room at the top of the lighthouse, the great dead lens filling the right half of the frame.
04Subject
Kneeling at its wick on the left third is the girl in the red knitted hood, a nine-year-old girl, small for her age, in a red knitted hood, an oversized rust oilskin coat to her shins and black rubber boots.
10Continuity
A spent match smokes between her fingers, and there is a smudge of soot on her left cheek. On the floor at her knee stands a small brass hand-lantern with one cracked green pane;
04Scale lock
inside it, the ember moth, a moth made of glowing embers, wings like cooling coals veined with thin gold, two black bead eyes, is just beginning to glow; its wingspan equals a child’s open hand, never smaller than a palm and never larger than a face.
05Moment
The moment: the match has just died; she has not yet noticed the glow at her knee.
07Light
Light: cold blue-grey moonlight through the lamp-room windows from the left; the moth’s small orange glow from the lantern, low and right, warming only the underside of her face.
08Colour
Colours: slate and ink; the moth’s ember orange is the only warm colour.
11Locks
Absolutely no warm gold light anywhere in the frame.
12Avoid
Avoid: 3D render, CGI, photorealism, anime, plastic, text, letters, signatures, watermark, extra fingers, distorted faces.
