The technique on one page

Thirteen rules. Every credit is spent once.
  1. Story before credits. Nothing is generated until the story works on paper.
  2. Price the whole film first. Retake buffers per stage, and a reserve never spent.
  3. One model, one setting, one look. The look sentence goes into every prompt.
  4. Sheets before shots. A sheet with a scale bar for every character.
  5. Handles, never names. “The girl in the red knitted hood.”
  6. Twelve slots, every time. The order never changes, so a missing slot shows.
  7. Lint before you paint. Every prompt against the style lock and the ledger.
  8. Batches of four, judged at full size. Fix batch one’s fault in batch two.
  9. Fix in post before you repaint. A repaint needs its root cause first.
  10. When a result gets worse, take away. Remove the last addition.
  11. Music first, then cut on it. Tempo, phrases, hits and silences, measured.
  12. Paintings hold; life comes in post. Video only where motion is the story.
  13. Deliver everything, and prove it. Loudness, sync, and a hashed archive.
