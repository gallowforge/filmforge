# The Lantern Moth: screenplay

36 seconds, three scenes, six shots. Wordless except two handwritten lines. Timings are provisional until the score is measured; the shot list is then retimed so every cut lands on the music.

## S1. The Dark (0:00 to 0:12)

**Music:** a low drone, a soft kick on each bar. Cold and alone.

**s01** (0:00, 6 s, extreme wide). The lighthouse on its black headland, the great lamp dead. Far out, a tiny boat with one red sail pitches in the swell. Snow crosses the frame.
*Handwritten:* "the lamp had gone out."

**s02** (0:06, 6 s, medium). The cold lamp room. The girl in the red knitted hood strikes her last match at the wick; it dies in a puff of smoke and leaves soot on her cheek. In the empty brass lantern at her feet, something begins to glow: the ember moth.

## S2. The Choice (0:12 to 0:24)

**Music:** plucked notes enter; the theme. Warmth, then the pull of the sea.

**s03** (0:12, 6 s, close). The moth on her fingertip, its light on her face. She cups it to her chest and smiles for the first time.

**s04** (0:18, 6 s, medium). Through the salt-crusted glass, the red sail is almost on the rocks. She looks from the moth to the sea. The music holds its breath (0:23.5). She opens her hands toward the lamp.

## S3. The Light (0:24 to 0:36)

**Music:** the hit at 0:24, then warm full chords.

**s05** (0:24, 6 s, extreme wide). The beam blazes out gold across the sea on the hit. The boat turns away from the rocks.

**s06** (0:30, 6 s, extreme wide; the same composition as s01). Dawn. The lamp warm, the boat safe in the cove, a hundred small ember moths resting on the lamp glass.
*Handwritten:* "some lights are only kept by giving them away."

**Title** in code over the last two seconds of s06.
