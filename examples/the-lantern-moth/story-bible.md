# The Lantern Moth: story bible

A worked example for the FilmForge method. Fictional, 36 seconds, six painted shots.

## Premise (one sentence)

On a black winter coast, a lighthouse girl whose great lamp has died must give away the only warm thing she has, a moth made of embers, to bring a lost boat home.

## Thesis

Some lights are only kept by giving them away.

## The heart (the six engines)

| Engine | In this film |
| --- | --- |
| One relationship | The girl and the ember moth: the first warm thing she has held all winter. |
| One flaw, with a price | She wants to keep the warmth for herself. While she cups it, the boat drifts toward the rocks. |
| One choice that breaks your heart | She opens her hands toward the dead lamp and lets the moth go into it. |
| One twist | The moth does not burn out. At dawn the lamp glass is covered in a hundred small ember moths. |
| One mirror | Her own reflection in the lamp lens: alone and lit by the moth in s03, then lost in the beam's light in s05. |
| One image, twice | s01 and s06 are the same composition: the dead lighthouse at night, then the warm lighthouse at dawn. |

## The world

A single rock headland in deep winter. The lighthouse is the only building. The sea is never calm. There is no blue in the sky until dawn, and no warm gold anywhere until the lamp is lit.

## Characters

| Handle (used in prompts) | Look | Wants | How they move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **the girl in the red knitted hood** (Nell, 9) | Small for her age, a red knitted hood, an oversized rust oilskin coat to her shins, black rubber boots, a smudge of soot on her left cheek from s02 on | Warmth. Not to be alone with the dark. | Small, careful, shoulders up against the cold; one big decisive gesture (opening her hands) |
| **the ember moth** | A moth made of glowing embers: wings like cooling coals veined with thin gold, two black bead eyes, a faint trail of sparks | To be a light for someone | Flickers rather than flies; settles, pulses with the music |

Names never go into a prompt: an image model can paint a name as text, and a name carries no visual information. Handles do.

## Props and states (carried into the continuity ledger)

- **the brass hand-lantern** with one cracked green pane: the girl carries it from s02 to s04. It is empty and cold; the moth hides in it.
- **soot on her left cheek** from the failed match in s02, through to the end.

## Setups and payoffs

| Planted | Paid off |
| --- | --- |
| The dead lamp in the first frame (s01) | The same frame at dawn, the lamp warm (s06) |
| The empty lantern she carries everywhere (s02) | It stays behind on the floor once the moth is gone (s05 onward, no lantern) |
| One moth on her fingertip (s03) | A hundred moths on the glass (s06) |
| No warm gold anywhere (s01 to s04) | The beam blazes gold (s05) |

## Colour script

| Scene | Time | Colours | Feeling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 The Dark | 0:00 to 0:12 | night slate, ink blue-grey, sea grey; one ember-orange spark at the very end | cold, alone |
| S2 The Choice | 0:12 to 0:24 | slate and ink, the moth's ember orange lighting her face | warmth held close, then the pull of the sea |
| S3 The Light | 0:24 to 0:36 | beam gold, dawn rose, pale morning blue | release, wonder |

Rule: no warm gold before s05. The only warm colour before the lamp is lit is the moth's ember orange.

## Production rules

- Paintings hold like film frames; life comes from code (sea spray, snow, the moth's flicker, the beam's sweep).
- Every cut lands on the score. The score's held breath (0:23.5 to 0:24) is the moment she lets go; the lamp lights on the hit at 0:24.
- Two handwritten narration lines, no voice: s01 and s06.
